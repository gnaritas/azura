Our story

A Rome kitchen, on a New York lake.

Azura began as the clubhouse of the Lakeshore Yacht & Country Club in Cicero — renovated this year into a restaurant of nearly 300 covers that looks straight out over Oneida Lake.

  1. The clubA lakeshore clubhouse. The building sits on the Cicero shore of Oneida Lake, part of the Lakeshore Yacht & Country Club. Building owner Guy Hart spent about $500,000 renovating the clubhouse for the restaurant, the syracuse.com opening report says.
  2. The partnersThe Inka’s team, at the lake. Jose Avalos-Rios and Alejandro Gonzalez — two of the four partners behind Inka’s and Margarita’s in Syracuse — took on Azura, with Andrew McDermott and Sebastian Ramirez running day-to-day operations.
  3. The kitchenA chef from Rome. Chef Luca Cardente grew up in Rome; the menu is built around the dishes he grew up with — handmade pasta, a carbonara finished at your table, Roman-style pizza, fresh seafood.
  4. 2026First the members, then everyone. For several months Azura served only country-club members, who tested dishes and gave feedback. It opened to the public on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

The name

The name Azura refers to the sky-blue look of Oneida Lake, Gonzalez told syracuse.com — the color of the water the dining room overlooks.

The room itself is big by Upstate clubhouse standards: nearly 200 seats in the main dining room, nearly 300 across the whole space, with two bars. The restaurant is the first piece of a planned lakeshore redevelopment — owner Guy Hart has said shops and apartments are coming to the property down the road.
