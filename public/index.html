Open daily from noon · Roman-inspired Mediterranean · Cicero, NY

The color of the lake. The cooking of Rome.

Azura is the Roman-inspired Mediterranean restaurant inside the renovated clubhouse of the Lakeshore Yacht & Country Club, looking out over Oneida Lake. The kitchen — led by Chef Luca Cardente, who grew up in Rome — makes the dishes he grew up with: handmade pasta, Roman-style pizza and fresh seafood. Opened to the public November 3, 2026, after the menu was previewed with club members for months.

Short answers

Before you come

When are you open?

Every day from noon: until 9 p.m. Monday through Friday, until 10 p.m. on weekends, as reported by syracuse.com at the public opening in November 2026.

Do I need a reservation?

Dinner reservations open on Resy, and the restaurant answers (315) 715-6895.

When did Azura open?

Azura opened to the public on November 3, 2026, after several months serving country-club members, who tested the menu and gave feedback first.

What kind of food do you serve?

Roman-inspired Italian-Mediterranean cooking from Chef Luca Cardente, who grew up in Rome: handmade pasta, rigatoni with vodka sauce, a carbonara finished at your table, Roman-style pizza and fresh seafood, including sea bass and salmon plates.

Is it really on the lake?

Yes — the dining room overlooks Oneida Lake from the renovated clubhouse of the Lakeshore Yacht & Country Club in Cicero, New York. The restaurant is named for the lake's sky-blue color.
