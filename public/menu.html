The menu

Pasta, pizza and the sea — the way Rome does them.

Azura’s menu is Roman-inspired Mediterranean cooking, built around the dishes Chef Luca Cardente grew up with in Rome. Handmade pasta, Roman-style pizza and fresh seafood pour alongside wine and the two bars. The full menu, with prices, is at the restaurant.

About the kitchen

The cooking starts in Rome. Chef Luca Cardente grew up there, and the menu is built from the dishes he grew up with — pasta made by hand, a carbonara finished at your table, Roman-style pizza — laid over the Mediterranean’s other traditions, seafood included.

Before opening to the public on November 3, the menu ran for months in front of Lakeshore Yacht & Country Club members, who tested dishes and gave feedback. “We have tested our menu, and we feel like we have what the community wants,” partner Alejandro Gonzalez told syracuse.com.
